# Per KPI Context

When generating hypotheses:
- Use KPI semantics (what it measures, typical drivers, and reporting caveats).
- Consider whether movement is structural or cyclical.
- Map at least one transmission channel (policy, external demand, credit, commodity, labor, demographics).
- Prioritize hypotheses that can be corroborated by observable events or policy actions.
- Keep narratives top-down: headline movement first, then driver decomposition, then implication.
- Avoid volatility language unless oscillation is sustained or decision-relevant.

GDP growth (KPI 3) specific:
- Build L2 depth by linking aggregate growth to available driver splits in the payload.
- If available, decompose growth through oil/non-oil, sector split, and expenditure components (private consumption, fixed investment, exports, imports).
- If component data is unavailable, keep attribution at aggregate level and state the limitation.
- Preserve real-vs-nominal discipline: do not use nominal price moves as proof of real-output contribution.
